PIXEL MONSTERS - MILESTONE 03 / VERSION 0.3.0.0

Double-click "Play Pixel Monsters.bat" or "Pixel Monsters.exe".
The cinematic director begins automatically. Two pristine 1,000-cube
monsters fight without combat input. Music follows damage and defeat.
Keep the EXE, PCK and bundled DLL together. Godot is not required to play.

V: director/free camera. Movement or RMB takes over the current shot.
WASD: fly. Z/X: rise/drop. Q/E: turn. RMB + mouse: look.
Shift: boost. +/-: camera speed. Wheel: lens. C: home view.
Space: pause/resume (free camera remains usable).
L: cycle 1x / 0.5x / 0.25x. R: new fight.
H: hide all HUD. F1: help. F3: diagnostics. M: music. K: shake.

The aftermath and rubble persist until R. There is no automatic restart.
The development project remains D:\Godot\Projects\Pixel-Monsters.
